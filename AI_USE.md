## How you worked with them

### My involvement

I directed compliance with the repository brief and glossary, constrained paid inference, chose the development environment and controlled submission priorities. I required targeted repairs, source preservation, deterministic money checks, regression coverage and fail-closed autonomous safety. I explicitly rejected practice-specific hardcoding, broad rewrites once the existing architecture was working, and global authority relaxation to chase an action score. I required existing successful caches to be reused and kept alternative providers confined to development.

Implementation, test execution, document inspection and artifact verification were carried out with coding-assistant tools under these instructions. I do not claim to have manually authored or individually inspected every implementation change. Final acceptance and submission remain my responsibility; the logs distinguish my directions from assisted execution.

### Suggestions were checked rather than treated as authority

A proposed remedy could not simply match a public answer: it needed a generic source/policy justification and regression coverage. Missing-document requests were separated from price adjudication without weakening signed-recipient or threshold gates. Exact identifiers and monetary tokens remained mandatory when repairing citation formatting. Invalid responses and provider failures were retained instead of being presented as successful runs. Public practice keys were inspected outside the harness for diagnosis; the harness neither reads nor embeds them. Exam artifacts were generated by the report/sheet functions, not manually answered.

### Failures found during development

Testing exposed duplicate invoice ownership across originals/resends, normalized ledger matching failures, unsupported calculation provenance, weak authority assertions, forged feedback provenance and provider cache contamination. Further cases exposed OCR identifier ambiguity, signing-date and sender parsing defects, lost revision history and a document-request action mismatch.

The Antigravity CLI truncated input near 192 KB. Lossless compaction retained documents and page text; completed-stream parsing recovered concatenated structured answers and allowed a legitimate null pickup citation. A separate, smaller exam request exceeded its eight-minute timeout without an assistant answer. Its exact retry allowance was exhausted. Development recovery partitioned only that scope, retained authoritative and linked evidence, reused successful caches and used a fallback after actual Claude quota exhaustion. An incomplete portfolio stream was treated as an untrusted proposal and independently reviewed, never accepted as a final cached answer.

Exam review also exposed numeric tokens joined to kg/% units, escaped whitespace in exact quotes and an incorrect leading citation fragment. Generic repairs require the original value and an exact same-document match. Independent scanned-invoice verification caught a model plan that divided INR by FX before the runtime's own conversion; it required targeted semantic repair rather than manual output editing. The fresh custom run also exposed public-schema assembly rejecting a null optional period on an invoice proposal. Final cleanup reproduced this with a deterministic regression and omitted null periods during public report assembly, preserving non-null values. The affected eval passed a cache-only replay with no new model calls. Arithmetic, source checks and policy remain code-controlled. Confidence and semantic completeness still have limits; public scores do not prove hidden correctness.

Final verification: the exam report and answer sheet were generated through the report/sheet functions; independent Decimal replay and original-source scanned-invoice checks passed. Validation passes 9/9 and all 115 local tests pass. The fresh custom run completed once at 16/19, with two transport interruptions and one report-schema failure, recorded separately from semantic mismatches. No practice replay or paid API call was made in this completion pass. The schema failure is now fixed and the affected case passes from cache. The full-run result remains historical; neither transport failure was rerun and no full-run score was manually rewritten.
